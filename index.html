Why this research matters

🎯

The problem

Most lip-reading datasets are built from BBC and TED footage. Their speakers don't reflect the diversity of real people, so models can quietly perform worse for some groups.

🔬

What we measure

We compare recognition accuracy across self-reported skin tone, gender, age, facial hair and glasses, plus measured lighting, to find where bias appears.

🤝

Why you matter

Every clip adds a real, diverse speaker. Your recordings help us measure bias honestly and test ways to reduce it.

Tips for a great recording

  • 💡
    Face the light. A window or lamp in front of you, not behind.
  • 📱
    Hold steady at eye level. Prop your phone up if you can.
  • 👄
    Keep your mouth visible. No hands, masks or mic in front of it.
  • 🗣️
    Speak at a natural pace. Clear, normal volume, no need to exaggerate.
  • 🤫
    Find a quiet spot. Background noise makes the speech check harder.
  • 🌐
    Use Chrome or Edge for automatic speech checking. Safari works too.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is recorded?

A 640×480 video of your face at 25 fps with audio, up to 12 seconds per sentence. We also save your form answers, the sentence text, the speech transcript, average face brightness and basic device info (browser and camera resolution).

Why do you need my skin tone, gender and age?

The whole point of CLARUS is to check whether lip-reading accuracy differs between groups. Without these labels we can't measure fairness. They are self-reported and you choose the answer.

Why was my clip rejected?

Each clip is checked in your browser: if less than half of the sentence's words were heard, nothing was heard, or inappropriate language was detected, the clip isn't uploaded and you'll be asked to record again. Read the sentence exactly as shown in a quiet place.

The camera check won't turn green.

Make sure only your face is in view, move toward a light source facing you, and hold the device still for a moment. Very bright backlighting (a window behind you) is the most common cause.

Which browsers work?

Chrome and Edge on Android or desktop give the best experience, including automatic speech checking. Safari on iPhone works too; in browsers without speech recognition (e.g. Firefox) clips are uploaded and flagged for manual review.

Can I re-record a sentence?

Yes. After each upload you can tap Re-record This Sentence; the new clip replaces the old one.

How do I get my data deleted?

Contact the CLARUS project team with your name and roll number and we'll remove your folder from storage.
